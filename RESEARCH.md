# King Tool Inc. — redesign research

Live site reviewed 2026-11-23: https://kingmachines.com/ and every page in its footer nav (assembly, material handling, welding, machining, sawing, washing, inspection, stamping, marking, innovative solutions).

## Original brand (kept)

- Business: King Tool Inc., also King Tool and Die, also known as King Machines. Family owned. Adrian, Michigan.
- Line: “Machining & Assembly Solutions Since 1964.”
- Address: 971 Division Street, Adrian, Michigan 49221.
- Phone: (517) 265-2741 Voice. Fax: (517) 263-7578.
- Email on the live pages is printed as `sales(at)kingmachines.com` (obfuscated plain text). The redesign uses that same address as `sales@kingmachines.com` with a `mailto:` link.
- People named on the site: founder Larry (retired, still at the shop weekly); sons Gary and Dave manage the company. 15 full time employees. 30,000 square foot facility. OEM of industrial machinery.
- Palette from the 2011 RoboHELP pages: page field `rgb(51, 102, 255)` / `#3366ff`, title maroon `rgb(153, 0, 0)` / `#990000` in Impact, body `#333333` in Arial, nav `#003366`. The shop photo shows a maroon awning and a black crown. Those colors are the redesign palette. No teal and no brown lodge palette.
- Logo and machine photos downloaded from kingmachines.com into `assets/`.
- Dead links on the old site (Tripod HTTP, a `file:///C:/` path) were not carried over.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding copied.

### 1. Ahaus Tool and Engineering — https://ahaus.com/

Special-equipment and tool-and-die shop, close to King Tool’s actual work (assembly machines, robotics, machining), not a consumer tool brand.

Borrowed:

- Treat capabilities as a named set the visitor can open, instead of one long scrolling brochure.
- Put the machine photograph first and the job name directly under it (King Tool’s own captions).
- Keep a straight path to talk about a build.
- Stay in a tool-and-die / special-machine voice. Do not restyle the shop as a generic manufacturer landing page.

### 2. PEKO Precision Products — https://www.pekoprecision.com/

Contract builder of machinery, equipment, and assemblies.

Borrowed:

- Keep the phone number in the top bar on desktop, and give every capability its own page.
- Lead each capability page with the work (photo and the shop’s own description), not a stock slogan.
- Offer a short project-note form. On this demo the form submits nowhere and says so on the page.

### 3. Specialty Machine — https://www.specialtymachinelp.com/

Contract machining, welding, assembly, and test.

Borrowed:

- A short proof strip under the introduction, using only figures King Tool already publishes: since 1964, 15 full time employees, 30,000 square foot facility, Adrian, Michigan. No certifications, ratings, or equipment lists.
- Capabilities as a compact chip list, not tall padded cards.
- The headline states the work in plain industrial type: their own line, “Machining & Assembly Solutions Since 1964,” set under the building photo rather than on top of it.

Also looked at BT Machine (https://btmachineinc.com/) for the pattern of a captioned work grid and a closing contact block. King Tool’s grid uses only their photographs and captions.
